# Bash Level 2: First Steps

## Architecture / Rationale

This level covers the first commands you type in a terminal. You learn to move around, see where you are, and get help.

Scope:
- Your first Bash command: Hello World.
- Knowing where you are and what is around you.
- Moving between folders with absolute and relative paths.
- The structure of a command, flags, and arguments.
- Getting help with `man` and `--help`.

## Performance / Optimization Notes

- These commands are the foundation of everything else. Practice them until they feel natural.
- You will use `ls`, `cd`, and `pwd` hundreds of times a day. Learn them first.
