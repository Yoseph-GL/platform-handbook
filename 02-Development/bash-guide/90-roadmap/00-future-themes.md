# Bash Future Themes Roadmap

## Architecture / Rationale

This roadmap keeps the guide scalable without mixing advanced topics into the foundational levels.

Planned progression:
1. **Regular Expressions (Regex)**: patterns for `grep`, `sed`, and text processing.
2. **Sed and Awk**: stream editing and text processing languages.
3. **Arrays in Bash**: indexed and associative arrays for complex data.
4. **Advanced Scripting**: trap signals, debug with `set -x`, and write safe scripts.
5. **SSH and Remote Work**: connecting to servers, key management, and `scp`.
6. **Dotfile Management**: organizing `.bashrc`, `.profile`, and versioning your configs.
7. **Package Managers**: `apt`, `brew`, and installing software from the command line.

## Performance / Optimization Notes

- Keep advanced topics isolated by level to preserve entry-level readability.
